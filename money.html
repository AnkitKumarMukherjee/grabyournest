Frequently Asked Questions

Is this ebook only for people earning a high salary?

No. The framework is designed around your actual income and circumstances. The book uses different examples and explains that the amounts are illustrative rather than mandatory.

I am a student/freelancer. Is this still useful?

Yes. The guide covers variable income and situations where you are still building your financial foundation. Freelancers are specifically addressed in the emergency-fund and worked-example sections.

Does this ebook give stock tips or guaranteed investment returns?

No. It is not a stock-tipping or guaranteed-return product. It explains financial concepts and common beginner options at an educational level. Investment returns are not guaranteed.

Is this suitable if I have debt?

The book discusses debt, credit-card traps and prioritising high-interest debt. Your own repayment and investment decisions depend on your specific circumstances, interest rates and tax position.
