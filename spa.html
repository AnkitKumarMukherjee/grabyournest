The still & sage philosophy

Wellbeing is not a luxury. It is a way of coming back to you.

We believe the best kind of care begins with listening. Our spa is imagined as a gentle pause from the pace of everyday life: a place for considered treatments, quiet surroundings and unhurried moments.

From the first welcome to the final breath before you leave, every detail is designed to help you feel comfortable, cared for and at ease.
